# Lookup

The learner wants a quick recap of the core idea they learned before. This is
a convenience, not a search engine: look loosely, answer briefly.

## Steps

1. Read `INDEX.md`. Pick the cards that plausibly match the query, allowing
   for synonyms and other languages ("模數" may mean the card "Modular
   arithmetic"). If the index shows nothing likely, search `library/` and
   `lessons/` for a keyword or two. Stop there; do not search exhaustively.
2. **Found**: recap in a few sentences, leading with the learner's own summary
   (`## 用我自己的話`). Then list the card's links, and what links to it
   (search `library/` for `to: <id>`), so they can follow the idea to related
   concepts. Name the card and its `learned_in` lessons.
3. **Not found**: say in one line that there is no record, then answer the
   question directly.

## The one rule

Keep the learner's record and your own knowledge visibly apart. Whatever comes
from a card keeps its grade label; whatever you add from your own knowledge is
marked `model-only`. Being loose about *finding* is fine. Blurring *where a
statement came from* is not.

Do not edit cards or change grades here.
